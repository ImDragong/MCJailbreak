What is MC Jailbreak?

Minecraft Jailbreak (2.0) is a minecraft world created by a group of friends over a span of 4 years (and increasing) with in-depth lore, mechanics and play styles on a specific map called Jailbreak.

Inspired by the Roblox alternative, Jailbreak 2.0 was errected after its previous iterations were left behind to deliver a fresh platter of ideas into an action-adventure open world map with lots of memories.

This (newer git-based) website is the collection of all those ideas to forever remind of the greatness of Minecraft Jailbreak when it inevitably is left behind.
